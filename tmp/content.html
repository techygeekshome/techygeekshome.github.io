Storage: the easiest upgrade to justify

Crucial P310 1TB NVMe SSD: If your PC still boots from a hard drive or an old SATA SSD, this is the single biggest speed-up you can buy. Fast, cheap per gigabyte, and it fits any M.2 2280 slot.

Samsung T7 1TB portable SSD: Small enough for a pocket, quick enough to run backups in minutes rather than an hour. We use one for Windows install media and image backups.

Synology DS225+ 2-bay NAS: For anyone who wants proper backups at home. Pair it with two NAS drives and you have mirrored storage that backs up every PC in the house.

Networking: fix the Wi-Fi once and for all

TP-Link Deco X50 mesh Wi-Fi (3-pack): Most 'my internet is slow' problems are really 'my router is in the wrong room'. A mesh kit fixes dead spots without running cables.

TP-Link TL-SG108S 8-port gigabit switch: Cheap, silent and reliable. Wire the desktop, console and TV in one spot and take them off the Wi-Fi.

Klein Tools network cable tester: If you ever crimp your own cables or chase a flaky wall socket, this saves an afternoon of guessing.

Desk and work-from-home kit

Logitech MX Master 3S mouse: The mouse most IT people end up with. Quiet clicks, a scroll wheel that flies through long logs, and it works across two PCs.

Logitech MX Keys S keyboard: Low, quiet and backlit. Pairs with three devices, so one keyboard does the work laptop and the home PC.

Anker 8-in-1 USB-C docking station: One cable from the laptop to two monitors, network and USB. Worth it if you plug in and out every day.

Dell UltraSharp 27in QHD monitor: Sharp text, good colours and a stand that actually adjusts. A big step up from a basic 1080p screen.

Building or upgrading a PC

Corsair Vengeance 32GB DDR5 RAM: 32GB is the sweet spot for a modern Windows 11 build. Enough for gaming, browsers and a VM or two.

Corsair RM750e power supply: Fully modular and quiet. The one part you should never cheap out on, because a bad PSU can take other parts with it.

Streaming and the living room

Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K Max: Always one of the most heavily discounted items on the day. Fast, Wi-Fi 6 and good enough for most TVs.

Google TV Streamer 4K: The better pick if you live in Google's world and want something that sits behind the TV and stays out of the way.

Raspberry Pi projects

Raspberry Pi 5 Active Cooler: A must if your Pi 5 does anything heavier than sit there. Stops it throttling under load.

Samsung EVO Select microSD card: Cheap Pi cards fail. A decent card is the difference between a Pi that runs for years and one that corrupts in a month.

How to tell a real Black Friday deal from a fake one

  • Check the price history. A "deal" that matches the price from three weeks ago is not a deal.
  • Compare the exact model. Bundles and "special editions" are often made just for the sale so you cannot compare them.
  • Watch for "Lightning Deals". They are real, but they sell out fast and the price comes back the next day.
  • For storage and RAM, the price per gigabyte matters more than the percentage off.
